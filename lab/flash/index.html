Dots

The name printed as a halftone screen — the dots are the letterform.

Colour

Every name turns the colour further round the wheel. White or black dots have no hue to turn, so with the defaults it is the aura that changes and the dots stay white — give the dots a colour and they will turn too. At zero the roll holds one colour.

Aura

Blurred, offset copies of the dot field beneath the dots — never the letterform, so no ghost of the type shows.
