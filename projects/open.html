OPEN is a practice-based gathering. We sit in silence for twenty minutes, then talk — each week around a theme like process, groundedness, craft, creativity, or technology. A timer holds the silence; a prompt closes it.

It borrows from council practice — a circle where you listen and speak from the heart — and from Suzuki's Zen Mind, Beginner's Mind.

No experience needed. Free, every week, never recorded.
