# Case study — The Quiet Leaf

*The demo narrative for the portfolio. The Quiet Leaf is a fictional client built
inside Press to show the full arc: system → creation → publishing. Every screenshot
is the real product.*

---

## The setup (portfolio intro text)

The Quiet Leaf is a small-batch loose-leaf tea shop in Logan Square — single-origin
teas, Saturday tastings, a reading hour on Sunday mornings. Their voice is calm and
a little literary; their imagery is steam, hands, and morning light.

They needed what every small brand needs: to post like they have a design team,
without having one. So we gave them Press.

## The arc (five beats — each beat = one section of the case study)

### Beat 1 — The system
> **We built their brand into the tool itself.**
> A forest-and-clay palette. Five named text styles — Display, Headline, Label, Body,
> Quote — specified down to the letter-spacing, with live previews. Their photography,
> curated into libraries: pours, leaves & tins, the shop, hands & cups. Even the
> interface wears their leaf.

*Screens: Brand Kit page (palette + text styles), photo libraries grid, the
Press × leaf header.*

### Beat 2 — The rails
> **Three templates, locked where it counts.**
> Tasting Notes for quotes. Weekly Steep for events. On the Menu for the teas
> themselves. The team edits the quote, the date, the price — never the type,
> never the grid.

*Screens: a template open in design mode showing the "For creators, this element is…"
panel; the same template in use mode with the fields form.*

### Beat 3 — The shortcut
> **"Announce Saturday's oolong tasting."**
> That sentence is the whole workflow. Press reads their library — actually looks at
> the photos — picks the golden-hour pour, writes the caption in their no-exclamation-
> points voice, and sets the type. The team refines and approves.

*Screens: the Compose page with the chosen photo + reason; the composed post open
in the editor.*

### Beat 4 — The volume
> **A month of mornings, drafted in one.**
> Their seasonal notes went into the Content Studio as a single paste. Out came a
> calendar of drafts — tasting announcements, steeping notes, quiet aphorisms —
> each one already in its template.

*Screens: Content Studio with content pasted + templates selected; the Drafts grid after.*

### Beat 5 — The send
> **Published from the same room it was designed in.**
> Scheduled to Instagram — single posts and carousels — from inside Press. The queue
> shows the week the way a menu shows the day.

*Screens: the schedule modal (carousel with slide strip); the Schedule queue page.*

## The closing line

> Press is what a brand system looks like when it's alive: the designer sets it,
> the client runs it, and nothing off-brand ever leaves the building.

## Detail captions (for image galleries)

- **Brand Kit** — Named text styles with live previews. The system, not a PDF of it.
- **Photo libraries** — Their own photography, curated. The AI composes only from these.
- **Press × The Quiet Leaf** — The interface re-dresses itself for every client.
- **Locked template** — The quote is editable. Nothing else is.
- **Create a post** — Described in a sentence; composed from their library, in their voice.
- **Version history** — Thirty steps of undo you can see.
- **Recreate** — A flat JPG from the archive, rebuilt into editable layers.
- **The queue** — Wednesday's post, already where it belongs.
