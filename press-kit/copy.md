## Descriptions

### Short (cards, meta descriptions — ~30 words)
Press is a white-label brand studio: designers lock the system — templates, type, color,
photography — and clients create, compose, and publish inside it. On-brand, every time.

### Medium (~70 words)
Every designer knows the heartbreak: you hand off a beautiful brand system, and six weeks
later it's stretched type and off-palette pink in somebody's Canva. Press ends that. It's
a studio where the designer locks the system — templates with editable fields, named text
styles, the client's own photo library — and the client creates freely inside it. AI writes
and composes in their voice; publishing to Instagram and Facebook happens in the same room.

### Long (case-study intro — ~140 words)
Press started as a question: what if the brand system *was* the tool? Not a PDF of rules
nobody opens — the actual place where a client's content gets made.

In Press, the designer sets the system: locked templates where only the right things are
editable, a brand kit with named text styles down to the letter-spacing, photo libraries
curated from the client's own imagery. The client's team works inside those rails — filling
fields, never breaking layouts.

Then the AI does the heavy lifting. Describe a post and Press finds the right photo from
the client's own library — never stock — writes the copy in their voice, and lays it out.
Paste a month of notes and the Content Studio drafts the whole calendar. When it's ready,
Press publishes it — single posts, carousels, scheduled straight to Instagram and Facebook.

One roof. Every client its own world. Nothing off-brand gets out.

## Feature callouts

### 1 · Locked templates
**Short:** Templates where only the right things are editable.
**Long:** The designer decides what moves: this line is a field, this mark is pinned, this
image is the photo slot. The team fills in the blanks — they can't stretch the logo, can't
move the type, can't break the grid. Freedom inside the rails.

### 2 · Brand Kit, down to the letter-spacing
**Short:** Palette, named text styles, photo libraries — the system, live in the tool.
**Long:** Not a style-guide PDF — the working system. Named text styles carry font, size,
color, leading and tracking, with live previews; they surface as one-click presets in the
editor. The client's photography lives in curated libraries. Even the interface wears the
brand: Press × their mark, in the header.

### 3 · Create a post
**Short:** Describe the post. Press finds the photo and sets the type.
**Long:** Tell Press what the post should say. It looks through the client's own photo
library — actually *looks*, with vision, never keyword-matching filenames, never stock —
picks the shot that fits the mood, writes the copy in the brand voice, and composes the
layout. You refine; it never starts from a blank canvas.

### 4 · Content Studio
**Short:** Paste a month of notes. Get a month of drafts.
**Long:** A content dump goes in — themes, quotes, announcements, half-sentences. Press
reads it against the client's templates and drafts a batch of on-brand posts, each one a
starting point a human approves. A month of content in an afternoon.

### 5 · Recreate from a screenshot
**Short:** A Figma export goes in. An editable template comes out.
**Long:** Years of designs living as flat JPGs? Drop one in. Press reverse-engineers it —
exact copy, closest fonts, colors, shapes, photo regions — and rebuilds it as editable
layers, with the original floating as a tracing overlay while you fine-tune. A back
catalog becomes a template library.

### 6 · Publish from the same room
**Short:** Instagram & Facebook — posts, carousels, scheduled.
**Long:** No exporting, no re-uploading, no third tool. Design it, caption it, schedule it —
Press publishes directly to Instagram and Facebook, carousels included, on the client's
calendar. The queue shows what's going out and what already went.

### 7 · A real editor underneath
**Short:** Version history, smart guides, background removal, duotones.
**Long:** A thirty-step visual history you can scrub. Smart guides and snapping. Out-of-frame
ghosts that show exactly what's cropped. One-click background removal, brand-colored duotone
effects, alignment tools, letter-spacing control. Small things, all of them sharp.

### 8 · Every client, one roof
**Short:** Multi-client by design — each brand its own world.
**Long:** Agencies run every client from one place. Each gets its own templates, brand kit,
photo libraries, AI voice, and publishing connections — and the interface re-dresses itself
per client. Switching brands feels like walking into a different studio.
